Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Adam Graphics ("we", "us") is a sole trader based in London, England. This page explains what personal information is collected through adamgraphics.store and how it's used.

What we collect

When you place an order, our payment provider, Stripe, collects your name, email address, delivery address and payment details directly on their own secure checkout page. We don't see or store your card details — Stripe processes payment on our behalf and only shares the order and delivery information we need to fulfil it (see Stripe's own privacy policy at stripe.com/gb/privacy).

If you email us directly, we'll have your email address and whatever you choose to include in that message.

What we do with it

We use your order details only to prepare, package and post your order, and to contact you if there's a problem with it. We don't sell your information or use it for marketing unless you separately ask to hear from us.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed for tax and accounting purposes, in line with UK HMRC requirements.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (where we're not legally required to keep it, e.g. for tax records). To do any of this, email adam.thirkettle123@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to adam.thirkettle123@gmail.com.
